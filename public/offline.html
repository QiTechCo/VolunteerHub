How to volunteer

  1. Create an on-domain account with your name, email, and a password.
  2. Add availability and role preferences on your profile.
  3. Sign up for a published shift. If a role is full, you join the waitlist.
  4. Hours post after staff confirm attendance.

How you can help

Canvassing. Neighborhood walks and doors. Each shift lists the meeting point and hours.
Poll greeting. Greet voters at the polling location named on the shift.
Yard sign posting. Place or pick up yard signs at locations staff publish on the shift.
Event hosting. Host a house gathering. Staff publishes the host shift.
Phone banking. Call through the list staff publishes on the shift.
Text banking. Send campaign texts from the list and tool named on the shift.
