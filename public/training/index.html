Volunteer Training Program

People. Power. Purpose.

A three-module training for volunteers on Dimple Ajmera’s campaign for Charlotte City Council At-Large. Learn the organizing skills that turn good intentions into votes, relationships and lasting change for Charlotte.

• 3 self-paced modules• Short videos + hands-on activities• Progress saves on this device

Welcome, volunteer

Thank you for joining the campaign to keep Dimple Ajmera working for Charlotte on City Council.

Campaigns are won by people: neighbors talking with neighbors, volunteers who show up and bring friends, and leaders who help others lead. This program teaches the organizing skills behind that work, adapted from a respected North Carolina organizer training for the day-to-day work of this campaign.

Every lesson opens with a short video (most under a minute), then a few key takeaways and hands-on activities. Go at your own pace, and open the full written lesson whenever you want more detail. Your progress and worksheet answers save automatically in this browser.

About the candidate

Dimple Ajmera

Democrat · Charlotte City Council, At-Large. Working mother, CPA and Charlotte City Council member since 2017. She came to the U.S. at 16, worked her way through USC, and managed multimillion-dollar budgets before turning to public service after her father’s sudden death.

Priorities: a safe Charlotte whatever your zip code · sustainable infrastructure · affordable housing · economic opportunity citywide.

How the modules work

🎬 Watch

A short video opens every lesson, followed by 3–4 key takeaways. The full written lesson is one click away.

🧩 Practice

Flip cards, sorting activities, scenarios and calculators you can try as you read.

✎ Reflect

Worksheets for your story, your 1:1 plan, your leadership declaration and more. They save automatically.

✓ Check

Each module ends with a knowledge check. Score about 80% or better to earn your completion badge.

Ground rules for representing the campaign

  • Use the facts in this training or on dimpleajmera.com. If you don’t know an answer, say so and pass the question to staff.
  • Follow the current script, data-entry instructions and safety guidance from your field organizer.
  • Be courteous to everyone, including people who disagree. Thank them and move on.
  • Direct voting questions to the Mecklenburg County Board of Elections.
